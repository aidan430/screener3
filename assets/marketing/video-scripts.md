# Needle in the Haystack: 5 short video scripts

For TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Upright 9:16, 15 to 30 seconds each. Every
shot is something that really happens in the game, so the videos never promise what players
won't find.

**Recording tips**
- Record in Roblox Studio (Play) or a live server. Studio's camera and screen recorder both work.
- To reach later moments quickly, use the Studio-only testing switches in
  `src/shared/ConfigCosmetics.luau` (`Config.Testing`). Turn them off again before publishing.
- Put the most exciting second at the very start. People decide to keep watching within about
  two seconds.
- Big captions sit in the middle of the frame, away from the app's own buttons at the edges.
- Pick a trending upbeat sound in the app, or use the game's own music and sound effects.
- Post one video a day. Reuse the same clips with different hooks, and keep whichever does best.
- Never promise free Robux or prizes, and don't ask viewers for personal details.

## 1. "1 needle. 5,000,000 straws."  (20 s)

**Hook (0–2 s):** a slow pull-back from the hay to show the whole giant haystack. Big text:
**THERE'S 1 NEEDLE IN HERE.**

| Time | Shot (what to record) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Camera close on the hay, pulling back to the full 80-stud haystack | THERE'S 1 NEEDLE IN HERE |
| 2–5 s | First person: lift hay with the trowel. The "+6" floats up | 5,000,000 STRAWS |
| 5–9 s | Fast cuts: better forks, the combo climbing (x14, x20 GREAT!) | Upgrade your fork… |
| 9–13 s | Climbing stairs up the side of the stack, then digging in at the top | …climb up and dig in |
| 13–17 s | The red thread story beat and its camera move ("Follow the thread") | Follow the clues |
| 17–20 s | Freeze on the haystack, needle sparkle (thumbnail style) | Could YOU find it? |

**Caption:** Only 1 needle in 5,000,000 straws. How long would it take you? 🪡🌾 #roblox #robloxgames

## 2. "LEVEL UP every few seconds" (15 s)

**Hook (0–2 s):** LEVEL UP! filling the screen with confetti (a 10th level).

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | A LEVEL 10! moment with confetti | (none: let it play) |
| 2–6 s | First person with the XP bar in view, lifting fast; the bar fills and glows | Every lift = XP |
| 6–9 s | "x2 XP!" pops by the crosshair (a Lucky Lift) | Lucky Lifts = x2 XP |
| 9–12 s | Another LEVEL UP!, the Strength line in view | Get stronger, carry more |
| 12–15 s | The Top Farmers board by the farm entrance | Can you top the board? |

**Caption:** The most satisfying farming game on Roblox? 🌾⬆️ #roblox #robloxsimulator

## 3. "Day 1 vs Day 30" (25 s)

**Hook (0–2 s):** a split screen. Left: a tiny trowel scooping a handful. Right: a farm full of
machines eating the haystack. Text: **DAY 1 vs DAY 30**.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Split screen: trowel / full farm | DAY 1 vs DAY 30 |
| 2–6 s | The trowel, slow, a few straws at a time | Day 1: a garden trowel |
| 6–10 s | Pitchfork, then a better tool, each lifting more ("+" numbers growing) | Better tools… |
| 10–15 s | Building on your plot: stairs, an escalator, a conveyor carrying bundles | …build your farm… |
| 15–21 s | Machines and generators running, the heap shrinking from the outside in, the trader's basket filling | …and let the machines dig |
| 21–25 s | Wide shot of the farm and the half-eaten stack (the "Halfway" cinematic) | Where are you on day 30? |

**Caption:** From one trowel to a hay factory 🚜 #roblox #robloxtycoon

## 4. Story: "Grandma lost her needle" (30 s)

**Hook (0–2 s):** the opening storm cinematic sweeping over the giant stack, with the title
card **The storm**.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text / voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Opening cinematic: widescreen bars, the camera sweeping in | Last night's storm… |
| 3–8 s | Grandma Mabel's dialogue: "somewhere in there is my silver needle" | (her line on screen) |
| 8–12 s | Patch the talking scarecrow: "Oi! Mind where you're digging!" | Meet Patch 😄 |
| 12–17 s | Finding the red button, then the torn note ("Found in the hay") | Every clump hides something |
| 17–23 s | Pip the crow's nest, or Clem's horseshoe bet | Who will find theirs first? |
| 23–30 s | The haystack from far away at sunset; end on the needle sparkle | Help Grandma find it ▶️ |

**Caption:** Grandma's silver needle is somewhere in this haystack… 🪡👵 #roblox #robloxstory

## 5. "The moment you find it" (20 s)

**Hook (0–2 s):** the needle-found camera circling the haystack, with the banner
**YOU FOUND THE NEEDLE!**

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | The needle cinematic: a slow circle round the stack | THE MOMENT YOU FIND IT |
| 2–7 s | Rewind feel: quick cuts of hours of digging, stairs, machines, the heap shrinking | after 5,000,000 straws… |
| 7–11 s | The last clump: dig, the clump pops, the needle appears | (silence, then the pop) |
| 11–15 s | YOU FOUND THE NEEDLE! with confetti, and Grandma's happy line | 🎉 |
| 15–20 s | A cosmetic on show: the Golden Straw Crown or a title above a head | Show it off 👑 |

**Caption:** It took forever… but it was worth it 🪡✨ #roblox #robloxgames
